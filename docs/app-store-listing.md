```
enMirror turns your iPhone into a clear, well-lit mirror. Free, with no ads, no subscriptions, no account and no tracking.

RING LIGHT
Light up your face with a glowing frame around the screen. Pick a warm or cool tone and the size of the light; the screen goes to full brightness and goes back when you leave.

A CLOSER LOOK
• Zoom up to 3× with a pinch or the slider; double-tap for 1×.
• Brighten or darken the image, and tap to meter on a spot.
• Freeze the mirror to look closely, then pinch and pan the still.

AS OTHERS SEE YOU
Flip the mirror to see yourself the way other people do.

SEE THE BACK OF YOUR HEAD
The delayed mirror shows you what happened 2, 3 or 5 seconds ago. Stand the phone up, turn around, and look back to check your hair.

EVEN ON BOTH SIDES
A centre line, a 3×3 grid or a face oval helps you match brows, liner and contour on both sides.

BEFORE AND AFTER
Pin a photo you took as a small tile over the live mirror to compare as you go.

HANDS FREE
• Use a volume button as the shutter.
• Set a 3 or 10 second self-timer.

OPEN IT IN A SECOND
• Controls for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button.
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
• Ask Siri, or touch and hold the app icon to open the mirror with the light on, the delayed mirror, the flipped view or the guide.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
enMirror works offline and sends nothing anywhere. The camera image stays on your iPhone, and a photo is saved to your library only when you tap Save.

In English and Vietnamese.
```

## App Review Information

- Sign-in required: **off** (no account).
- Contact: Phuong Phung, phone, support@trinhvaphuong.com.

**Notes**

```
enMirror is a mirror app that uses the front camera. It has no account, no network requests, no ads and no in-app purchases. A real iPhone is needed: the simulator has no camera.
1. Open the app and allow camera access. The live mirror fills the screen.
2. Bottom bar, left to right: Light (ring light; its panel sets tone and size), Exposure, the shutter (freezes a still), Zoom, and As others see you (flips the image).
3. After the shutter: Share, Save (asks for add-only Photos access), Pin (floats the photo as a tile over the live mirror; drag it to a corner, tap to reopen, x to remove) and Delete.
4. Top right: the clock button turns on the delayed mirror (tap cycles 2, 3, 5 s and off). The grid button shows an alignment guide (tap cycles centre line, grid, oval and off). Touch and hold either button to pick a value from a menu.
5. Touch and hold the shutter to set a 3 or 10 second self-timer. The volume buttons also act as the shutter.
6. Top left (i): About, with support links and the version.
7. Outside the app: Control Center and Lock Screen controls ("Mirror", "Mirror + Light"), Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri ("Open the mirror in enMirror"), and quick actions when you touch and hold the app icon.
Not offered on Mac or Apple Vision Pro: the app is built and tested for the iPhone front camera only.
```
